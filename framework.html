The 6-Step System That Fills
Your Service Calendar

This isn't a generic marketing playbook. It's the exact framework we use to generate 15+ booked service calls every month for HVAC contractors — from building your online reputation to maximizing customer lifetime value.

Step 1

Digital Word of Mouth

Before you spend a dollar on ads, your online presence needs to work for you. When a homeowner Googles "AC repair near me," what they find in the first 10 seconds decides whether they call you — or your competitor.

Social Media Presence

Your Facebook and Instagram profiles are the modern equivalent of a yard sign — except they reach thousands of homeowners in your service area. We build a professional social presence that showcases your completed projects, team, and customer reviews.

Homeowners check your social media before they call. If your last post is from six months ago or your page looks abandoned, they move on. We keep your profiles active, credible, and engaging — so when they land on your page, they see a company they want to hire.

Google Maps & Local SEO

When someone searches "HVAC contractor near me" or "AC repair [your city]," Google shows a map pack of three local businesses. Being in that map pack is the single most valuable organic position for an HVAC company — it's free traffic from people who are ready to call.

We optimize your Google Business Profile — photos, services, posts, Q&A, and categories — then build citations across local directories so Google trusts your location and relevance. The goal: appear in the top 3 map results for your highest-value services.

Review Generation

Reviews are the #1 trust signal for homeowners choosing an HVAC company. A business with 200+ five-star reviews will outperform a competitor with 15 reviews — in ad performance, map rankings, and conversion rates.

We set up automated review request sequences that go out after every completed job — via text and email — so your review count grows consistently without your team having to remember to ask. More reviews means higher rankings, more trust, and more calls.

Step 2

Value-Based Offer

Running ads without a compelling offer is like pouring water into a bucket with a hole. The offer is what makes homeowners stop scrolling, pick up the phone, and choose you over the five other HVAC companies in their area.

The Dream Outcome

What does the homeowner actually want? Not "an HVAC repair." They want their house to be comfortable again — fast, without getting ripped off, and with a company they can trust. Your offer needs to speak to that outcome, not just list your services.

Example: "Same-day AC repair with a 100% satisfaction guarantee — or you don't pay" is an outcome. "We fix air conditioners" is a description. The first one gets clicks. The second one gets scrolled past.

Perceived Likelihood of Achievement

The homeowner needs to believe you can actually deliver. This is where reviews, project photos, certifications, and years of experience come in. Your offer page and ads need to stack proof so the homeowner thinks, "These guys know what they're doing."

We build this proof into every touchpoint — social proof badges, review counts, certification logos, and real project showcases — so your credibility is visible before they even read the fine print.

Time to Value

How fast can you solve their problem? Homeowners with a broken AC in July don't want to wait three days for a quote. Speed is a massive differentiator in HVAC — and your offer should emphasize it.

Example: "Free estimate within 2 hours" or "Same-day service — we'll be there by 5 PM" signals urgency and professionalism. We help you define and communicate time-to-value in every ad and landing page.

Effort & Sacrifice

What does the homeowner have to do on their end? The fewer steps, the better. Your offer should remove friction: "Call now — no diagnostic fee," "Book online in 30 seconds," "We handle the permit and inspection."

We design your funnels to minimize effort at every stage — one-click calls, short forms, instant booking — so the path from "I need help" to "appointment confirmed" is as short as possible.

Step 3

Targeted Paid Traffic

With your offer dialed in and your online presence credible, it's time to put your message in front of the right homeowners at the right time. We use two channels — Meta and Google — because they serve different buying intents.

Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)

Meta Ads are demand-generation — they put your offer in front of homeowners who aren't actively searching but match your ideal customer profile. They own a home in your service area, are in the right income bracket, and have shown interest in home improvement.

This is the channel that fills your pipeline for maintenance plans, seasonal tune-ups, system upgrades, and new installations — the high-ticket jobs that homeowners don't Google until a problem hits. We create scroll-stopping ads with strong visuals, proven copy, and lead forms that capture contact information in two taps.

Why it works for HVAC: A homeowner who sees your "$49 AC Tune-Up" ad in June books the maintenance before their system breaks down in August. You're solving a problem they didn't know they had — and building a relationship before the emergency call goes to a competitor.

Google Ads (Search & Local Service)

Google Ads capture demand — the homeowner's AC just broke, they're sweating, and they type "AC repair near me" into Google. You need to be at the top of that results page. Google Search Ads and Local Service Ads put you there.

We manage keyword targeting, ad copy, bid strategy, and conversion tracking — and we monitor cost-per-lead weekly so your budget isn't wasted on irrelevant clicks. We also set up negative keywords aggressively ("DIY," "free," "jobs") so your spend goes only to homeowners who are ready to hire.

Why it works for HVAC: When someone searches "emergency furnace repair" at 11 PM, they're not browsing — they're buying. Google Ads lets you capture that intent at the exact moment it happens. Combined with speed-to-lead follow-up (Step 5), this is the fastest way to book high-intent service calls.

Step 4

Conversion System

Getting clicks is only half the equation. Those clicks need to land on a page that converts — and in HVAC, that means a page designed to generate phone calls and form fills, not just "inform."

Landing Page Design

We build dedicated landing pages for each campaign — not your homepage, not a generic services page. A landing page has one job: convert the visitor into a lead. Every element — headline, images, form, CTA — is designed to move the homeowner from "interested" to "booked."

Key elements: Clear headline matching the ad promise. Click-to-call button above the fold. Short form (name, phone, zip). Trust badges and review counts. Project photos showing completed work. A strong call-to-action: "Get Your Free Estimate Today."

Mobile-First & Fast

Over 75% of HVAC searches happen on mobile. If your landing page takes more than 3 seconds to load, you lose half your traffic before they even see your offer. We build every page mobile-first, lightweight, and fast — because a homeowner with a broken AC in their living room is on their phone, not a desktop.

Tracking & Attribution

Every landing page has conversion tracking, call tracking, and form tracking installed — so we know exactly which ads, keywords, and audiences are generating calls, and which ones are wasting money. This data feeds back into Steps 2 and 3, so campaigns get smarter every month.

Step 5

Speed-to-Lead & Follow-Up

This is where most HVAC companies lose money. They spend $1,000 on ads, generate 30 leads — and call them back 4 hours later. By then, the homeowner already booked with someone else. Speed-to-lead is the single biggest lever in your conversion rate.

The 5-Minute Rule

Studies show that calling a lead within 5 minutes makes you 21× more likely to book the appointment versus calling after 30 minutes. In HVAC, where the homeowner is often in an urgent situation, that window is even tighter.

Our appointment setting team follows up with every lead within 5 minutes — by phone, text, and email — to qualify them and book a service call into your calendar. Your techs show up. We handle everything in between.

Multi-Touch Follow-Up Sequence

Not every lead picks up the first call. That doesn't mean they're not interested — it means they're busy. We run a multi-day, multi-channel follow-up sequence: call → text → email → call again — until we reach them or confirm they're not interested.

The sequence:

Minute 0: Instant text + call
Hour 1: Second call + text
Hour 4: Email with offer recap
Day 1: Morning call + text
Day 2: Follow-up email
Day 3: Final call + "last chance" text

This persistent (but professional) follow-up recovers 20–30% of leads that most companies would lose.

AI-Powered Automation

Where speed matters most (the first text and email), we use AI-powered automation to respond instantly — even at 2 AM on a Saturday. The homeowner gets an immediate response confirming their request, providing an estimated arrival window, and offering to schedule by text.

This isn't a chatbot experience. It's designed to feel human, handle common questions ("How much does a tune-up cost?" "Can you come today?"), and hand off to a live person when the conversation needs it.

Step 6

Retention & Lifetime Value

The most profitable HVAC companies don't just chase new leads — they maximize the value of every customer they've already served. A single AC repair customer is worth $200. That same customer on a maintenance plan, buying a new system in 8 years, and referring two neighbors? Worth $15,000+.

Post-Job Follow-Up

After every completed job, we trigger an automated sequence: a thank-you message, a review request, and — one week later — a check-in to make sure everything is working. This builds goodwill, collects reviews (feeding Step 1), and opens the door for upsells.

The ask: "Thanks for choosing [Your Company]. How's everything running? By the way, our Fall Tune-Up Special is $49 — want me to schedule yours?" One message. Two outcomes: a review and a rebooking.

Maintenance Plan & Seasonal Campaigns

Maintenance agreements are the backbone of HVAC recurring revenue. We build email and text campaigns that promote your maintenance plans to past customers — timed to hit before peak seasons (spring for AC, fall for heating).

Example campaign: "Summer's coming. Your AC hasn't been serviced since last year. Book your $49 tune-up before June 1 and get priority scheduling all summer." This reactivates dormant customers and fills your calendar during shoulder seasons.

Referral System

Happy customers refer friends — but only if you make it easy and give them a reason. We set up a referral program that rewards customers for sending you business: "$50 off your next service for every friend you refer who books."

The referral ask is built into the post-job sequence, so it happens automatically. No awkward conversations. No business cards to hand out. Just a text with a referral link the customer can share with a neighbor.

The Complete Picture

All 6 Steps, Working Together

Each step compounds the others. Reviews make your ads cheaper. Your offer makes your landing page convert higher. Speed-to-lead makes every ad dollar go further. Retention turns one job into a customer for life. This is the system — and it's built specifically for HVAC.
